Membership

Join our network of legal professionals

Membership Types

Full Member (정회원)

  • Korean Attorneys (Byeonhosa)
  • UK Solicitors & Barristers
  • CILEX Fellows
  • Dual Qualified Lawyers

Benefits: Voting rights, eligibility for office, full access to events.

Associate Member (준회원)

  • Foreign Lawyers (involved in UK-Korea work)
  • Patent Attorneys, Accountants
  • Translators/Interpreters in legal field

Benefits: Access to seminars and networking events.

Student Member (학생회원)

  • UK Law Students (LLB, BA Law)
  • GDL, LPC, BPTC, SQE Candidates
  • Korean Law School Students
  • Trainee Solicitors & Pupil Barristers

Benefits: Mentoring, career support, discounted fees.

Institutional Member (기관회원)

  • Law Firms
  • Legal Institutions
  • Related Organizations

Annual Membership Fees

Membership subscriptions are charged annually, not monthly. The current annual fee schedule is:

Full Member (정회원)

£30 per year

Associate Member (준회원)

£20 per year

Student Member (학생회원)

£5 per year

Membership applications are subject to approval under the Association's Constitution. The membership year runs from 1 January to 31 December, and annual membership renews on 1 January. For members joining part-way through the year, the first-year fee is calculated by calendar days remaining from the admission date (inclusive) to 31 December, divided by the actual number of days in that year (365 or 366), and rounded down to the nearest whole pound. The payment due date, cancellation process and any refund terms will be confirmed before payment. Members may choose to arrange a standing order after receiving verified Association bank details; a standing order is optional and is not a condition of membership. Institutional membership fees are agreed separately. Please contact us to apply; this website does not currently collect membership payments.
